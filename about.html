About Us

Land sales, house sales, consultancy, survey, development & estate management.

House Land & Property Consultant

We are a registered consultancy (CAC BN 3380655) offering a full range of real estate services in the Federal Capital Territory. We sell land and houses, and provide professional consultancy, real estate survey support, sales, property development, estate management and general supply.

Based at Shop 2b, Amma Plaza, Area 11, Abuja, we work with developers, estate projects, private sellers and buyers to deliver clear options and practical support.

Whether you need a plot of land, a finished home, survey assistance, management of an estate, or guidance on a development project — our focus is honest advice and reliable service from first enquiry to completion.
